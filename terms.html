1. What this covers

Clip Pipeline ("the application") is a private, single-operator tool. It has no user accounts, no sign-up and no public interface. These terms govern the operator's own use of the application and describe the basis on which it accesses third-party services.

2. Single operator, no third-party users

The application is used exclusively by its operator, on hardware the operator owns and controls. It is not offered, licensed or made available to anyone else, and no third party can create an account or submit content through it.

3. Third-party services

  • Twitch: the application reads publicly available clip metadata and clip video files for channels whose published terms permit clip creation.
  • YouTube: the application uploads video to the operator's own channel using the youtube.upload scope and nothing more. It does not read analytics, comments, subscribers or any other account data.

Use of those services remains subject to their own terms. Where a source creator's terms are more restrictive than this document, the source creator's terms take precedence and the application is not used for that creator's material.

4. Content and attribution

Every upload credits the source stream's title and URL in the video description, and adds original commentary. Nothing is published straight from a reupload, and every upload begins as private until the operator deliberately changes it.

5. No warranty

The application is provided as-is, for the operator's own use, with no warranty of any kind. It may fail, produce incorrect output, or stop working when a third-party API changes.

6. Limitation of liability

Because there are no other users and no service is sold, there is no party to whom liability could extend beyond the operator, who accepts the risks of running it.

7. Changes
